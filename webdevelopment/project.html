Accessibility and Social Media: ISLT 7310

Project Description

This presentation explores how social media can be used to enhance accessibility, engagement, and connection within a school library setting. It highlights best practices for creating inclusive content, designing posts that are readable and user-friendly, and using platform features, such as alt text, captions, and visual contrast, to ensure all users can participate. The project was developed with the long-term goal of creating an Instagram page for the library, focusing on strategies that make information welcoming and accessible to all stakeholders. By combining creativity with thoughtful design, the presentation demonstrates how social media can strengthen a library's reach and impact.

Reflection

Creating this project helped me understand how thoughtful design choices make social media more accessible for all users. I follow many library Instagram pages, and I've seen firsthand how doing social media well can benefit every stakeholder. This process clarified how I can build an inclusive, engaging library presence of my own.

Cataloging for Children and Young Adults: ISLT 7312

Project Description

This presentation examines current trends in cataloging children’s and young adult materials, with a focus on how evolving standards and user needs are shaping modern library practices. It explores shifts toward more inclusive subject headings, improved metadata for diverse formats, and strategies for enhancing discoverability in both physical and digital collections. The presentation also highlights the growing importance of representation, accessibility, and age‑appropriate classification as libraries work to better serve young readers. By analyzing these trends, the project emphasizes how thoughtful cataloging can strengthen equity, usability, and engagement across youth collections.

Reflection

Exploring trends in cataloging children's and young adult materials helped me understand how thoughtful classification supports discovery, equity, and representation. I realized how evolving standards and inclusive metadata can better serve young readers. This project strengthened my appreciation for cataloging as a vital part of creating accessible, engaging library collections.

Technology in Education Blog: ISLT 9467

Project Description

This blog explores the evolving role of technology in today’s classrooms and how digital tools can meaningfully enhance teaching and learning. It highlights practical strategies for integrating technology in ways that support student engagement, accessibility, and creativity. Throughout the blog, I reflect on emerging trends, evaluate tools that promote collaboration and critical thinking, and consider how thoughtful tech integration can empower both teachers and students. The project also allowed me to connect my own experiences with broader conversations in educational technology, strengthening my understanding of how digital innovation can shape more inclusive and dynamic learning environments.

Reflection

Working on this blog deepened my understanding of how technology can enhance teaching while also shifting my perspective on the balance between digital and paper‑based learning. Reflecting on tools and trends helped me connect research to practice and strengthened my ability to design engaging, accessible, and purposeful learning experiences.
