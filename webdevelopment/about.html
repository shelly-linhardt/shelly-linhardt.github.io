Educator Credentials

Hello! My name is Shelly Linhardt, and I’m in my seventeenth year of teaching secondary education. I’ve taught at both the middle school and high school levels, with experience in reading, science, and most recently family and consumer sciences. My favorite thing about teaching FCS is giving students opportunities to create something they can be proud of. Whether it’s a fresh‑baked batch of chocolate chip cookies or a hand‑sewn tote bag, their pride truly lights me up. I’m currently pursuing an Educational Specialist (Ed.S.) in Learning Technologies and Design at the University of Missouri. Outside of teaching, I’m a mom and a wife, and my family keeps me grounded and energized. My husband and I have three kids, and we love spending time outdoors, especially biking and walking the Katy Trail in Mid‑Missouri. I also work part‑time as a pharmacy technician, which provides a calm balance to the lively pace of the classroom.
